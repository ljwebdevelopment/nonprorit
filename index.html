How it works

From seed to harvest, in three stages

Sprout Fund keeps the process short and honest, the same way a good seed packet tells you exactly what to expect.

  1. 01
    Sow

    Apply in 10 minutes

    Tell us who you are and what you want to build. No business plan required—just a real idea and a clear ask.

  2. 02
    Grow

    Get funded and guided

    Approved founders receive a $100–$500 micro-grant plus founder guides and a check-in with our team to help you plan next steps.

  3. 03
    Harvest

    Build it and report back

    Launch your idea, put the grant to work, and send us a short update on what grew. Your story helps fund the next teen founder.

The grant, at a glance

Small money, real terms, no fine print

Every grant works the same way—read it like the back of a seed packet. If you're not sure whether your idea qualifies, apply anyway and we'll help you figure it out.
